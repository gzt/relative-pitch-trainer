How pitch collections work

Scale-relative mode uses DO–RE–MI–FA–SOL–LA–TI in major, and LA–TI–DO–RE–MI–FA–SOL in natural minor. Chromatic mode selects semitone offsets 0–11 from the tonic. The selected key is the DO/major-key reference. In minor mode, LA is the relative-minor tonic (nine semitones above DO). Melodic notes are placed in the nearest octave to the preceding note.
